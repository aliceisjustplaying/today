Privacy policy

today · last updated 11 October 2026

today is a personal dashboard run by Alice for her own use. Only one Google account, hers, is allowed to sign in. Anyone else who tries is refused and nothing about them is stored.

What it accesses

When the allowed user signs in with Google, today requests her basic profile (name, email address) and read-only access to her Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It reads upcoming calendar events solely to display them to her in the app. It never creates, changes or deletes calendar data.

What it stores, and where

The Google access and refresh tokens and a session record are stored in the app's SQLite database on Alice's own server. Calendar events are not stored on the server; the browser keeps a short-lived local cache so the page loads quickly.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. Google user data is not used to train AI models. Its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Signing out deletes the session. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account permissions; on request the stored tokens are deleted from the server.
